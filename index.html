About Sandeep Kondury

Sandeep Kondury is an Austin-based founder and early-stage operator. He is the founder and CEO of feat. — the marketplace for everything AI helps you build. You built it; feat. lets people sell it for you by connecting builders with affiliates. List your product, set your commission, and approve affiliates for co-promotion in minutes.

As Sr. Venture Associate at Startup Haven Ventures, he works with venture-scale founders on early-stage strategy, capital, and systems. His thesis centers on lingo leverage — how language and cultural adoption predict startup outcomes.

Previously, Sandeep built accelerator operations and venture pipelines at Astralabs, advised founders on financial models at Forecastr, and contributed to portfolio and founder support across Startup Haven's network. He is a TED Editor's Pick speaker and a University of Washington alum (Foster / Lavin entrepreneurship community).
