I · The Ideas

The Persistence of Narrative

After evaluating 1,500+ startups and deploying $10M+ in early-stage capital, one pattern kept surfacing: language moves first. The words a company teaches the world are a leading indicator — cultural traction arrives before the financial metrics catch up. These are the ideas I keep returning to.

i.

Lingo Leverage

The best companies don’t just build products — they coin the vocabulary their market thinks in. When your words become the industry’s words, every conversation is distribution. Language adoption predicts startup outcomes earlier than any dashboard.

ii.

Narrative-Market Fit

Before product-market fit, there is narrative-market fit — the moment a story starts spreading on its own. Cultural traction is measurable, and it compounds: employees, investors, and customers begin working on the company’s behalf.
